About Us

Blue Bells Public School, Sector 4, Gurugram — committed to nurturing confident, compassionate, and future-ready global citizens.

Our Mission

To provide a caring and stimulating environment that inspires curiosity, critical thinking, and a lifelong love for learning.

Our Vision

To shape resilient, empathetic, and innovative learners who lead with integrity and serve society.

Principal’s Message

At Blue Bells, education goes beyond the classroom. We value a balanced approach that blends academic rigor with sports, arts, and values education, ensuring every child thrives holistically.

Why Blue Bells

Holistic development, student-centered pedagogy, experienced faculty, vibrant co-curriculars, and a safe, inclusive campus culture.

Admissions

Begin your Blue Bells journey. Applications are open for Nursery to Grade XII.

Admission Process

  1. Submit the online enquiry and application form.
  2. School visit and interaction/assessment (grade-appropriate).
  3. Document verification and fee formalities.
  4. Orientation and onboarding.

Eligibility

Age criteria as per CBSE norms; previous academic records (where applicable); transfer certificate for inter-school admissions.
